## Interpretation notes

- Flight 1: he shared a debris map from records obtained through FOIA in November 2024. The map is not attributed to him as its creator.
- Flight 2: the tank animation is an illustrative render, not a validated fluid simulation or a confirmed failure diagnosis.
- Flight 3: his follow-up corrects “parallel” to “perpendicular.” The app includes the correction alongside the initial hypothesis.
- Flights 4, 6, 9 and 13: recovery-vessel tracks, intended targets and drifting-ship positions are not treated as touchdown coordinates.
- Flight 5: the additional location is the discarded hot-stage adapter. The author gives a ±0.5 km margin; it is not a vehicle landing.
- Flight 7: the detailed February thread proposes failure mechanisms and explicitly allows that they may be wrong. A readable thread mirror is used because the original could not be retrieved.
- Flight 8: T+9:30 is the author’s approximate timing from stabilized footage. It is not a frame-perfect synchronization with the mission timeline.
- Flight 9: the initial post’s speed estimate was corrected later. The app omits the speed instead of repeating the superseded number.
- Flight 11: the Starbase overlay imagines a future tower approach. Ship 38 actually returned to the ocean.
- Flights 12–14: coordinates preserve the published digits. Decimal map markers are mathematical conversions, not claims of greater positional accuracy.

Post text was checked through the public FxTwitter status API when X did not serve readable pages. Relevant images and animation frames were inspected for Flights 1, 2, 8, 10 and 14. The app links canonical posts where available; no analysis images or videos have been bundled.
